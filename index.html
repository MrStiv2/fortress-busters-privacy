Privacy Policy

Fortress Busters: Merge Siege · CampStudio
Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use Fortress Busters (Android package com.campstudio.fortressbusterslegacy). The game is intended for a mixed audience, including children under 13. It has no CampStudio accounts, login, chat or social features.

Information used by the game

  • Local game data: progress, settings, rewards, and purchase-entitlement records are stored on your device. CampStudio does not operate a cloud-save service for this game. Android device backup or transfer features may separately preserve app data according to your device settings.
  • Advertising: we use Google AdMob for rewarded and interstitial ads. Its SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers such as app set ID, device/app information, ad and app interactions (such as launches, taps and video views), and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, and prevent fraud. Ad personalization depends on applicable consent and privacy settings.
  • Consent: Google's User Messaging Platform helps determine and record applicable advertising consent choices, using device/request information and consent status.
  • Purchases: Google Play Billing, integrated through Unity In-App Purchasing, processes purchases. Product IDs, purchase tokens/receipts, transaction IDs and purchase status are used to complete, acknowledge and restore eligible purchases and avoid duplicate rewards. Google handles payment details; the game does not receive your card number or banking credentials. Parents can manage purchase approvals and family payment settings through Google Play.
  • Online time checks: versions with daily-gift time verification request an HTTPS response from Google to read its date header. Google receives the IP address and normal request metadata; the request does not upload your game save.
  • Notifications: optional game reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in Android notification settings.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location. Approximate location can still be inferred by advertising services from your IP address. We do not include a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service; advertising SDKs still process their own diagnostic and interaction information.

Third-party services

Third-party providers handle information under their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — including AdMob, consent services, Google Play and online time requests; how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice — payment processing.
  • Unity Privacy Policy — the provider of the game engine and in-app purchasing integration.

Your advertising choices

Where required, Google's consent flow asks for advertising choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings (look under Privacy or Google → Ads; names vary by Android version). Older Android versions may offer “Opt out of Ads Personalization.” See Google's advertising ID guidance. These controls do not remove all ads or stop all technical, contextual or fraud-prevention data processing.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you clear the game's storage or uninstall it, subject to device backup/restore behavior. In Android Settings, open Apps → Fortress Busters → Storage → Clear storage/data to remove local progress and settings. This can permanently erase progress. Uninstalling does not erase Google's purchase records, cancel purchases, or delete data held by advertising providers.

Google and other providers retain their records under their own policies, including legal, payment and fraud-prevention requirements. Use their privacy controls or contact them for requests concerning their data. CampStudio cannot directly erase records held independently by Google. For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent directly to CampStudio, email us below. Correspondence is retained as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations; do not send payment credentials.

Security

Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypts its data in transit using TLS. Google Play handles billing through its services, and online time requests use HTTPS. Local saves use the device's app storage. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children and parents

The game is directed at a mixed audience including children. We do not ask children to provide names, email addresses or other personal details, and the game has no accounts, chat or social features. Technical information processed by advertising providers is described above; this is not a promise that no personal data is processed.

Our requirements for child-directed releases are Google AdMob child-directed treatment, non-personalized ads, maximum ad content rating G, and no interest-based advertising. These requirements must be implemented and verified in each release; this policy does not establish that an older version has those settings.

Parents or guardians may email campstudiomail@gmail.com with questions or requests to review or delete information supplied directly to CampStudio. Please identify the game and explain the request without sending sensitive information. We will review requests and address applicable rights under children's privacy laws, including COPPA and the GDPR where relevant. We do not claim that a privacy policy alone supplies parental consent or establishes compliance. Local data can be cleared as described above; requests about Google-held information are subject to Google's controls and policies.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
